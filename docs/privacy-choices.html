Privacy choices

Your data, your control.

CVee is designed to keep your working documents local, with deletion controls inside the app.

Delete local CVee data

Open CVee → Settings → Delete all data. This clears the data CVee stores in the app. Deleting the app also removes its local app data.

Delete exported copies

For PDFs, Word files or backups you saved outside CVee, delete the copies from the destination you selected, such as Files or iCloud Drive, and from any accounts or recipients you shared them with.

What you can revoke

Notifications can be disabled in iOS Settings. Face ID/passcode app locking can be disabled in CVee Settings. Apple Intelligence availability is controlled by the device and its Apple Intelligence settings.

Need help?

There is no CVee server account to close. For support questions, use CVee Support. For privacy questions, contact cvee@bakatel.co.uk.
