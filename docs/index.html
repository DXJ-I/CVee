Privacy by default

No CVee account. No CVee cloud.

CVee stores your CV, letters and applications locally. The app uses iOS file protection for its JSON data and can be locked with Face ID or the device passcode.

Full privacy policyPrivacy choices
What happens to your data?

When you import, CVee reads a temporary copy and removes it. When you export or share, the copy goes only to the destination you choose. Local reminders stay on the device. Device backups, Files/iCloud Drive and recipients of exported documents are outside CVee's control and have their own terms.

Apple Intelligence

Writing assistance that starts from your facts.

Where supported and enabled, CVee uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Suggestions are reviewed in-app, and the code validates figures and skills against the CV or vacancy text before applying changes.

UK-first

Built around practical UK conventions.

CVee checks for UK spelling patterns, role dates, concise profile length, cover-letter sign-offs and one-to-two-page CV guidance.
