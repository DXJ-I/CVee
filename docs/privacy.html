Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

At a glance: CVee does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service or CVee cloud. The app is designed so your working documents remain on your device unless you choose to export/share them or they are included in a device backup.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how CVee handles information in the CVee iPhone and iPad app. CVee is published by Adedeji Bakare.

2. What CVee stores

CVee stores the information you choose to enter or import, including CV profile details, cover-letter content, job-application records, saved CV versions, settings and local reminder dates. These records are stored in the app’s local storage. The app writes its document data using iOS file protection.

3. What CVee collects

CVee does not collect personal data for CVee servers or maintain a user account. The current app contains no CVee analytics, advertising SDK, tracking system or remote database. App Store privacy declarations should therefore be completed as Data Not Collected, provided the shipped build and integrated third-party code continue to match this policy.

4. Apple Intelligence

CVee’s writing assistance, vacancy matching, career-coach features and some import assistance use Apple’s Foundation Models framework where the device supports Apple Intelligence and the feature is available. CVee is designed to use the model on-device. CVee does not receive the model’s input or output on a CVee server.

5. Files you import

When you import a PDF, Word, RTF or text file, CVee reads a temporary copy for parsing and removes the temporary copy after processing. Imported content becomes part of the local CV data only when you choose the resulting changes.

6. Exports and sharing

When you export to PDF or Word, or use the system share sheet, the resulting file is placed in a temporary location for the export/share operation and then cleaned up by the app. The destination you choose — such as Files, iCloud Drive, Mail, Messages, a recruiter, a job board or another app — is outside CVee’s control and is governed by that destination’s own terms and privacy practices.

7. Face ID / Touch ID / passcode

Optional app locking uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication APIs. CVee does not receive or store biometric data. Authentication is performed by the operating system.

8. Notifications

Interview and follow-up reminders are scheduled locally using Apple’s notification framework. CVee does not run a reminder server.

9. Backups

Your device’s backup system may include app data depending on your device and backup settings. When you deliberately create a CVee backup and save it to Files or iCloud Drive, that file is then subject to the privacy and security practices of the location where you save it.

10. Retention and deletion

CVee keeps local data until you change or delete it. Settings → Delete all data removes the data CVee stores in the app. Deleting the app also removes its local data, subject to any copies you deliberately exported or included in device backups.

Because CVee does not maintain a server-side account or server copy of your CV, there is normally no CVee server record to request deletion from. If your concern relates to an exported file or backup stored elsewhere, deletion must be handled with that destination provider.

11. Children

CVee is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children through a CVee server.

12. Third parties

CVee relies on operating-system services supplied by Apple, including Foundation Models, LocalAuthentication, UserNotifications and iOS file protection. CVee does not add third-party advertising or analytics services.

13. Changes to this policy

If CVee’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the App Store privacy information will be reviewed before release of the changed version.

14. Contact

Support contact: cvee@bakatel.co.uk. Update this address before publishing the App Store listing.

This policy is written for the current CVee 1.7 / build 15 project and is not legal advice.
